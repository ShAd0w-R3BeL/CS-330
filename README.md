Portfolio README: CS 330 Project Reflection
How do I approach designing software?
My approach to software design has transitioned from a task-oriented mindset to a holistic, architectural one. This project required me to move beyond simply writing code that functions to crafting an environment that is spatially and logically coherent.

New Design Skills: This project sharpened my ability to conceptualize 3D space, specifically regarding lighting, camera transformations, and the mathematical relationships between vertex data and rendered output.

Design Process: I utilized an iterative process: beginning with wireframe primitives, establishing the spatial hierarchy, and finally applying texture and lighting shaders to bring the "scene" to life.

Future Application: The tactical approach of "incremental visualization"—building a basic structure before detailing it—is a strategy I intend to apply to back-end architecture, where defining clear interfaces and data structures must precede implementation of complex business logic.

How do I approach developing programs?
Development is no longer about linear completion; it is a cycle of refinement. Navigating the complexities of OpenGL taught me that managing the pipeline effectively is just as important as the logic within the scene.

New Development Strategies: I adopted a modular approach to shader management and object transformation, which made debugging individual scene elements significantly easier than a monolithic code approach.

Role of Iteration: Iteration was critical. For instance, addressing the positioning of the coffee mug handle necessitated a deep dive into vertex transformation matrices, teaching me that small changes in mathematical input have profound impacts on visual output.

Evolution of Approach: Throughout the milestones, I moved from feeling overwhelmed by graphics math to comfortably manipulating coordinate systems. My approach has evolved to prioritize maintainability, ensuring that code is commented and organized for future revisions.

How can computer science help me in reaching my goals?
Computer graphics and visualization represent the intersection of pure mathematics and practical application, providing a visual feedback loop that is invaluable for a student of Computer Science.

Educational Pathway: These skills have bridged the gap between theoretical linear algebra and practical implementation. This knowledge is directly applicable to future coursework in simulation, UI/UX design, or data visualization for my work in Statistics.

Professional Pathway: Understanding how to visualize data and environments is a universal asset. Whether working on complex system architectures or front-end interface development, the ability to translate conceptual models into functional, navigable visualizations is a high-value skill that I will carry into my future career in software engineering.
